General rules

These apply to every event. Event-specific rules are inside each event.

Eligibility and documents
  • Open to PUC Commerce and Science students.
  • A valid college ID card is mandatory for every participant.
  • A letter from the Principal or HOD with the student list is required.
  • A student may take part in more than one event.
Faculty and consent
  • Participants are under 18: each college sends one accompanying faculty member for every 10 students.
  • Parental consent is the responsibility of the sending college.
Conduct and judging
  • Malpractice, misconduct or unfair help may lead to disqualification.
  • Maintain discipline and decorum throughout the fest.
  • Mobile phones are not allowed during rounds unless the event says otherwise.
  • Judges’ and organising committee’s decisions are final and binding.
  • The committee may change rules, format or schedule if required.
  • Late arrival can cause clashes; organisers are not responsible for them.

Organising Committee

Faculty committees from the Department of Commerce and Management and the Department of Computer Applications.
